About the College

D.Y. Patil College of Engineering & Technology is a well-known autonomous institute located in Kolhapur. Established in 1984, the college provides quality education in engineering and technology fields. It is approved by AICTE and recognized by the Government of Maharashtra.

The institute has NAAC 'A' grade accreditation and focuses on academic excellence, research activities, and overall development of students.

Departments

  • Computer Science & Engineering
  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Civil Engineering
  • Electronics & Telecommunication
